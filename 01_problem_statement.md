# 1. Problem Statement

Urban traffic congestion and delayed emergency response are critical challenges in modern smart cities.
This system provides a unified, real-time database platform to monitor vehicle movements, manage traffic
signals, detect and respond to road incidents, and coordinate emergency dispatch across multiple city zones.
It integrates relational, object-relational, NoSQL, spatial, temporal, distributed, and XML-based storage
to cover all advanced DBMS concepts in a single coherent architecture, enabling data-driven decisions
that reduce average emergency response time and optimize signal throughput city-wide.
